## Overview

Gestor Aramis brings content planning, production materials and review into one workspace. Marketing coordinates the plan; design can open a production queue with the script, footage, deadline and correction notes together.

I defined the product around our team's workflow, prioritized a clear interface and used colleague feedback to guide changes. Implementation was heavily assisted by AI coding tools. The internal V1 is fully adopted by the team.
